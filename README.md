# Ex.No.-1.1-Design and Draft the given 2D Sketches in modelling software.

## AIM

To sketch the given drawing with dimensions using fusion 360 tool.

## REQUIREMENTS

  1. Autodesk fusion 360
  2. Windows 10
  3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 2 GB or more)
  4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 
  
## PROCEDURE

1. Create a Sketch: Click Create Sketch on the top toolbar and select the Front (XZ) plane.
2. <img width="502" height="611" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-16 222749" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8dfde75e-51f2-4444-918b-30c59992751a" />

3. Draw Center Circles: Draw two concentric circles at the origin for the main center hub and the inner bore.
4. <img width="592" height="491" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-16 020302" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9d8abe26-b67c-48f6-9ad7-9598f16ace43" />

5. Create Center Splines: Sketch a small circle or slot on the inner bore wall and use Circular Pattern to duplicate it six times, then Trim the excess lines to form the internal spline teeth.
6. <img width="227" height="326" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-16 020603" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/704a49ee-3d82-4169-b43c-4b5b71eba4f7" />

7. Draw One Lobe: Above the origin, draw two concentric circles to form the top mounting arm and its through-hole.
9. Connect the Lobe: Draw two lines tangent to the outer center circle and the outer top circle, then Trim the overlapping inner arcs.
10. Pattern the Lobes: Select the top arm geometry and use Circular Pattern around the center origin with a quantity of 3
11. <img width="710" height="562" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-17 212157" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ee66841f-97ab-42b4-8578-2d43045575df" />

12. Add Side Notches: Draw three small circles on the outer edges between the lobes and use Trim to create the semi-circular cutouts.
13. <img width="227" height="326" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-16 020603" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dfc756ed-c054-4042-a2a5-5997c5d9c36e" />

14. Extrude the Base: Click Finish Sketch, select the main profile, and use Extrude to give the part its base thickness.
